# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Warehouse managers, inventory operators, and stock coordinators who need to reconcile imported stock spreadsheets, track product history, and manage stock status in high-volume, time-sensitive contexts.

## Product Purpose
To provide a clean, high-density dashboard and utility for stock reconciliation (relying on Sorensen-Dice fuzzy matching) and intuitive historical stock tracking/visualizations, ensuring minimal manual effort and maximum speed.

## Brand Personality
Efficient, Precise, and Organized (focused on clean utility, crisp tables, and structured states). Look and feel should resemble modern developer/productivity tools like GitHub or Linear.

## Anti-references
Cluttered or multi-nested cards, tiny low-contrast gray text on off-white backgrounds, distracting gradient text, and oversimplified layouts with excessive empty whitespace requiring constant scrolling.

## Design Principles
- **Data over Decoration**: Prioritize information density and readability. Avoid decorative elements or layout padding that reduces scannability.
- **Structured Density**: Organize multi-step tasks (like imports and reviews) with clean sub-borders, clear focus states, and tab-based layouts.
- **Proactive Assist**: Leverage Sorensen-Dice fuzzy matching suggestions with prominent, easy-to-apply inline cues, building operator trust via high feedback clarity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Focus on WCAG AA contrast (≥ 4.5:1), keyboard-navigable data tables, clear outline rings on active focus states, and prefers-reduced-motion alternatives for any micro-animations.
